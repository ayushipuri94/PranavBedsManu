Getting to Suraburdi Meadows, Nagpur

Destination

Suraburdi Meadows

A lakeside resort on the Nagpur–Amravati highway, about 13 km from Nagpur city. All celebrations happen here.

Open in Google Maps

By air

Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar International Airport (NAG)

Nagpur's airport is in Sonegaon, about 8 km southwest of the city centre, with direct flights from most major Indian cities. From the airport, take a cab to the venue.

By train

Nagpur Junction (NGP)

The city's main railway station, well connected across India. Cabs and autos are available outside the station.
